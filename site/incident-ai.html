5.2 / AI-Assisted Response

Accelerate evidence and execution under human command

The incident, objectives, severity rules and recovery criteria are the same as 5.1 Runbook-Led Response. AI reduces time spent gathering evidence, coordinating information and preparing actions. Authority remains with the Incident Commander and owning teams.

Integration responsibilities

MCP integrations provide scoped access to tools and evidence; skills encode versioned procedures; workflows coordinate approvals, execution and verification. None of these alone establishes that a production action is authorized or safe. Read access, recommendation, approval and execution are separate permissions.

For this incident, an evidence assistant can assemble affected-tenant counts and enrichment/backlog trends, identify the owning on-call, and prepare a supported mitigation proposal. At the next maturity level, an approved workflow might apply an existing concurrency limit to a scoped cohort, observe customer impact, and roll back if stop criteria are crossed. That action is permissible only if accepted work remains recoverable and the control has been tested.

If the assistant or a connector fails, continue paging, command, communication and recovery through the conventional process. AI must not become a new dependency for operating the incident.

Execution authority depends on 5.3 Automation Maturity. Existing domain accountability remains in 3.1 Domains and Ownership.
